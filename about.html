About

Mindfully Blind is the natural result of what happens when relationships drive artistry. Made up of three siblings, an in-law, and a childhood best friend, our love for each other and our craft is reflected in the quality of our music and performances. As we grow on our journey, we invite you to come on in; whether you’ve been wandering for a while or been here before, make yourself at home.

Antonio

Antonio is our lead vocalist, rhythm guitarist, and collective little brother (and quite literally Kristofer and Olivia’s youngest brother). As frontman of the band, you can count on him to create a fan experience that draws you in and keeps you in until the very last chord.

Kristofer

Kristofer is our bass extraordinaire, backup vocalist, and big brother (Antonio and Olivia’s literal big brother). At our gigs, you’ll see him stomping around the stage and headbanging with those luscious locks.

Olivia

Olivia is our synth, backup vocalist, and sister (Antonio and Kristofer’s literal sister). She brings the vibes with specially curated sounds that elevate the band’s signature alternative style to the next level.

Dan

Dan is our lead guitarist, audio engineer, and in-law (Olivia’s husband, Antonio and Kristofer’s brother-in-law). His attention to detail and analytical ear bring the band’s sounds to life both on stage and in the studio.

Jackson

Jackson is our drummer. As a chosen member of the family, he is the keystone of the band’s energy with his exceptional skill and innate sense of rhythm. You might need to stretch before and after our gigs; his drumming makes it nearly impossible not to headbang along with us.
